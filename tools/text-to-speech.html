Online Text to Speech Tool

Text to Speech converts written text into spoken audio using speech capabilities available through your browser or device. It can be useful for listening to written material, checking how sentences sound when read aloud, practicing pronunciation, or consuming text without continuously reading from the screen.

How to Use Text to Speech

Enter or paste the text that you want to hear into the available input area. Select the available voice or speech settings when supported, then start playback. Available voices, languages, pronunciation, and speech quality can vary depending on your browser, operating system, and installed speech services.

Why Listen to Written Text?

Listening can provide another way to review written content. Writers may use speech playback to notice awkward sentences or repeated words that are easy to overlook while reading. Language learners may also find spoken output useful when exploring pronunciation and listening to short passages.

Browser and Voice Compatibility

Text-to-speech functionality depends on technologies supported by the browser and device. As a result, the list of available voices may differ between computers, phones, and operating systems. For critical pronunciation or accessibility requirements, verify the generated speech using an appropriate dedicated service or native accessibility feature.
