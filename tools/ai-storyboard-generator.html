What Is an AI Storyboard Generator?

An AI storyboard generator helps turn a video concept into a sequence of planned scenes. Each scene can include an action, camera shot, camera movement, duration, transition and a visual prompt. This gives creators a structured roadmap before producing a video.

How to Create a Video Storyboard

  1. Enter your main video idea.
  2. Choose the number of scenes.
  3. Select the duration for each scene.
  4. Choose a visual style and aspect ratio.
  5. Select the language and camera movement.
  6. Add optional details such as setting, lighting, characters or visual requirements.
  7. Click Generate Storyboard.
  8. Review each scene and copy individual scenes or the entire storyboard.

What Is Included in Each Scene?

  • Scene title: Identifies the role of the scene.
  • Action: Describes what should happen.
  • Camera: Suggests a shot or camera perspective.
  • Movement: Provides camera movement guidance.
  • Duration: Specifies the selected scene duration.
  • Transition: Suggests how the scene can connect to the next one.
  • AI Video Prompt: Combines visual style, subject, action, camera and production details.

Aspect Ratios for Different Video Platforms

9:16 Vertical is useful for vertical short-form content. 16:9 Landscape is commonly used for traditional widescreen video. 1:1 Square can be useful for square social posts, while 4:5 Portrait provides a taller social-media format.

Why Plan a Storyboard Before Making a Video?

A storyboard makes it easier to visualize a sequence before production. It can help organize actions, camera perspectives, timing and transitions so the final video has a clearer structure. You can also revise individual scenes before spending time creating the final visuals.

Using Storyboards for AI Video Creation

The generated prompts are designed as creative starting points for AI video workflows. Review and customize each prompt for the specific video generator you use, especially when you need consistent characters, locations, objects, lighting or camera behavior across multiple scenes.

Does This Tool Require an API Key?

No. The current version generates storyboard prompts locally in the browser without an API key. The generated prompts can then be adapted to your preferred AI video workflow.
