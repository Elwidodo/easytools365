Convert Text to PDF Online

Text to PDF is a convenient way to turn written content into a portable document. PDF files are useful for saving, sharing, printing and archiving notes, drafts, instructions, simple reports, educational material and other text-based content.

EasyTools365 creates the document directly in your browser, so you can convert text without installing a desktop PDF application.

How to Convert Text to PDF

  1. Enter an optional document title.
  2. Type or paste your text into the text area.
  3. Choose A4 or Letter page size.
  4. Select the font size and page margin.
  5. Click Generate & Download PDF.
  6. Save the generated PDF for sharing, printing or storage.

Why Convert Text to PDF?

PDF is widely supported on computers, phones, tablets and web browsers. Converting text to PDF can make written information easier to distribute and store in a consistent document format.

  • Save notes as a portable document.
  • Create simple reports and instructions.
  • Prepare educational or study material.
  • Share text in a familiar document format.
  • Print written content more conveniently.
