What Is a Percentage Calculator?

A Percentage Calculator is an online tool that helps you calculate percentages quickly without doing the formulas manually. It can find a percentage of a number, determine what percentage one number is of another, calculate percentage change, and apply a percentage increase or decrease.

The EasyTools365 Percentage Calculator is useful for everyday calculations involving discounts, prices, statistics, grades, business figures, and other percentage-based values.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To find a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide the result by 100.

Result = (Percentage × Number) / 100

For example, 20% of 150 is 30 because (20 × 150) / 100 = 30.
