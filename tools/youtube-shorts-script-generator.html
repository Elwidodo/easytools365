What Is a YouTube Shorts Script Generator?

A YouTube Shorts script generator helps creators structure short-form videos before recording or producing them. A structured script can organize the opening hook, main message, voice-over, scenes, visual direction, on-screen text and ending CTA.

EasyTools365 creates a starting script from your topic, duration, style, language, platform and visual preferences so you can adapt it to your own channel.

How to Generate a Shorts Script

  1. Enter your video topic.
  2. Select the video duration from 15 to 60 seconds.
  3. Choose a script style such as Viral, Educational or Storytelling.
  4. Select English or Indonesian.
  5. Choose the target platform and visual style.
  6. Select an ending CTA or choose No CTA.
  7. Add optional production details.
  8. Click Generate Shorts Script and review the scenes.
  9. Copy individual scenes or use Copy Full Script.

Tips for Better Shorts Scripts

  • Start with a hook that immediately communicates the video's subject or benefit.
  • Keep the script focused on one primary idea.
  • Match the visual prompt to what you can actually produce.
  • Use on-screen text to reinforce important points without overcrowding the frame.
  • Customize generated narration so it sounds natural in your own voice.
  • Verify factual claims and examples before publishing.
