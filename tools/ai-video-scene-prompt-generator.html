What Is an AI Video Scene Prompt Generator?

An AI video scene prompt generator helps turn a specific scene idea into a structured text prompt. Instead of describing everything in one sentence, you can separate the subject, action and environment, then control the camera, lighting, mood, duration, aspect ratio and visual quality.

How to Create an AI Video Scene Prompt

  1. Enter the Main Subject.
  2. Describe the Subject Action.
  3. Describe the Environment or location.
  4. Choose the visual style.
  5. Select the camera shot and movement.
  6. Choose lighting and mood.
  7. Select aspect ratio, duration and quality.
  8. Add optional extra details.
  9. Click Generate Video Prompt.
  10. Copy the prompt and adapt it for your preferred AI video generator.

Why Separate Subject, Action and Environment?

Separating these elements makes it easier to describe a scene clearly. The subject identifies what matters most, the action explains what happens, and the environment establishes where the scene takes place. Together, these details provide a stronger foundation for a consistent video prompt.

Camera Shot and Camera Movement

Camera controls influence how the scene is presented. Close-ups and macro shots emphasize details, wide shots show the environment, and overhead or low-angle shots can change the sense of scale. Push-ins, pull-outs, pans, tracking, orbiting and zooms provide additional motion direction.

Lighting and Mood

Lighting and atmosphere help establish the visual character of a scene. Cinematic, soft, dramatic, studio, natural, neon, golden-hour and moody lighting can create very different results. Mood choices such as satisfying, peaceful, mysterious, energetic, luxury and epic further define the intended feeling.

Aspect Ratio and Duration

The tool supports 9:16 vertical, 16:9 landscape, 1:1 square, 4:5 portrait and 21:9 cinematic formats. You can also choose 5, 8, 10 or 15 seconds. Select the combination that fits the platform and content format you are creating.

What Is a Negative Prompt?

The generated negative prompt lists common unwanted visual problems such as flickering, jitter, warped anatomy, duplicate objects, random text, logos, watermarks and compression artifacts. It can be copied separately so you can use it in an AI video workflow that supports negative prompts.

Does This Tool Generate the Video?

No. EasyTools365 AI Video Scene Prompt Generator creates the text prompt only. You can copy the generated prompt and use it with an AI video generator or another creative workflow.
