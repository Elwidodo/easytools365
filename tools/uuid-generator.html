UUID v4 Explained

UUID version 4 uses randomly generated bits to create the identifier. A UUID is 128 bits and is normally displayed as 32 hexadecimal characters separated by four hyphens.

UUIDs have an extremely low probability of collision when generated correctly, but “unique” should not be interpreted as an absolute mathematical guarantee.
