What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates your age from your date of birth. Instead of manually counting years, months and days, you can get an instant result in your browser.

The EasyTools365 Age Calculator also provides total months, total weeks, total days and the number of days remaining until your next birthday.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Select or enter your date of birth.
  2. Click Calculate Age.
  3. Your age will appear in years, months and days.
  4. Review the additional age statistics below the main result.

No registration or installation is required.

Why Use an Online Age Calculator?

Calculating an exact age manually can be confusing because months have different numbers of days and leap years affect date calculations. An online calculator makes this process faster and easier.

  • Calculate exact age instantly.
  • See years, months and days.
  • View total months, weeks and days.
  • See the time remaining until your next birthday.
  • Use the tool for free without an account.
