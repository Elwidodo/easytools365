What Is an AI Image Prompt Generator?

An AI image prompt generator helps turn a simple visual idea into a more detailed text prompt. A well-structured prompt can describe the subject, visual style, composition, lighting, mood, quality and other details that may help an AI image generator interpret the intended scene.

How to Use the AI Image Prompt Generator

  1. Describe the image you want to create.
  2. Select your preferred AI image platform.
  3. Choose a visual style, aspect ratio and composition.
  4. Select lighting, mood and detail level.
  5. Add optional extra details or things to avoid.
  6. Click Generate Image Prompt.
  7. Copy the generated prompt into your preferred AI image generator.

Why Use Detailed Image Prompts?

Detailed prompts can make your creative intention clearer by describing multiple visual elements in one structured request. This can be especially useful when creating concepts for illustrations, social media graphics, product visuals, cinematic scenes, characters, environments or design references.

Prompt Options Available

  • AI Platform: Universal, DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion and Leonardo AI.
  • Visual Style: cinematic, anime, photorealistic, 3D, fantasy, illustration, product photography and minimalist.
  • Composition: wide, medium, close-up, macro, aerial, low-angle and top-down.
  • Lighting: cinematic, daylight, golden hour, neon, studio, dramatic and moody.
  • Mood: cinematic, cozy, epic, dreamy, mysterious, peaceful, energetic and dark.
