What Is a YouTube Thumbnail Text Generator?

A thumbnail text generator helps content creators brainstorm short wording ideas for YouTube video thumbnails and Shorts. Thumbnail text can communicate a topic, benefit, emotion, result or point of curiosity in just a few words.

Because thumbnails are usually viewed at small sizes, concise text can be easier to scan than long sentences. Use the generated ideas as creative starting points and adapt them to the actual content of your video.

How to Generate Thumbnail Text

  1. Enter your video topic.
  2. Choose a thumbnail style such as Viral, Curiosity or Minimal.
  3. Select English or Indonesian.
  4. Choose how many ideas you want.
  5. Add an optional important keyword.
  6. Click Generate Thumbnail Text.
  7. Copy individual ideas or use Copy All.

Tips for Better YouTube Thumbnail Text

  • Keep the wording concise and readable on small screens.
  • Use strong words only when they accurately describe the video.
  • Make the thumbnail text complement the video title instead of simply duplicating it.
  • Prioritize one clear message rather than several competing messages.
  • Test different concepts and choose the wording that best matches your audience and content.
  • Avoid misleading claims or clickbait that does not reflect the actual video.
