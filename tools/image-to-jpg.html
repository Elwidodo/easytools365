What Is an Image to JPG Converter?

An Image to JPG Converter changes a supported image into the widely compatible JPG format. JPG is commonly used for photographs, websites, social media, email attachments and general image sharing.

How to Convert an Image to JPG

  1. Select or drag a PNG, WebP or JPG image into the upload area.
  2. Adjust JPG Quality if needed.
  3. Click Convert to JPG.
  4. Review the converted image.
  5. Click Download JPG.

Why Convert Images to JPG?

JPG is supported by a wide range of websites, devices and applications. Converting an image can be useful when a service does not accept the original PNG or WebP format or when you need a broadly compatible image file.

JPG Quality and File Size

The quality slider controls the JPEG output quality. Higher values generally preserve more visual detail but may create a larger file. Lower values generally create smaller files but can introduce more visible compression.

Transparency and JPG

JPG does not support transparent backgrounds. When a PNG or WebP image contains transparency, this converter renders transparent areas on a white background before creating the JPG.

Local Browser Processing

The conversion uses browser image and canvas APIs. Your selected image does not need to be uploaded to the EasyTools365 server, so the conversion can happen locally in your browser.
