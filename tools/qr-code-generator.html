    What Is a QR Code?

    A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that can store information such as website addresses, text, contact details and other data. When scanned with a compatible smartphone or QR code reader, the stored information can be accessed quickly.

    QR codes are commonly used on websites, product packaging, business cards, menus, posters, advertisements, event materials and many other forms of printed or digital communication.

    How to Create a QR Code

    1. Enter the URL, text, phone number, email or other information you want to share.
    2. Choose the QR size and colors if needed.
    3. Click Generate QR.
    4. Review the generated QR code and its information.
    5. Download the QR code as a PNG or copy it.
    6. Test the QR code with a smartphone before publishing or printing it.

    Common Uses for QR Codes

    Website Links

    Share a website or landing page without requiring users to type a long web address.

    Business Information

    Place QR codes on business cards, brochures, posters and promotional materials.

    Events and Menus

    Provide quick access to menus, schedules, registration pages or event information.

    Digital Sharing

    Move information from printed material or another screen to a smartphone quickly.

    Tips for Creating Better QR Codes

    • Use strong contrast between the QR code and its background.
    • Do not make the QR code unnecessarily small when printing it.
    • Test the QR code with more than one smartphone when possible.
    • Make sure the encoded URL or information is correct before publishing.
    • Keep enough empty space around the QR code for reliable scanning.
