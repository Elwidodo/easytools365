What Is a QR Code?

A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that can store information such as website addresses, text, contact information and other data. When scanned using a compatible smartphone or QR code reader, the stored information can be accessed quickly.

QR codes are commonly used on websites, business cards, menus, posters, advertisements, event materials, product packaging and other printed or digital communication.

How to Create a QR Code

  1. Enter the URL, text, phone number, email address or other information you want to share.
  2. Choose the QR size and colors if needed.
  3. Click Generate QR.
  4. Review the generated QR code.
  5. Download the QR code as a PNG image or copy it.
  6. Test the QR code with a smartphone before printing or publishing it.

Tips for Creating Better QR Codes

  • Use strong contrast between the QR code and its background.
  • Avoid making the QR code unnecessarily small when printing it.
  • Test the QR code with a smartphone before publishing it.
  • Make sure the encoded URL or information is correct.
  • Keep enough empty space around the QR code for reliable scanning.
