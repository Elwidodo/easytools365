What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online utility that analyzes written text and reports useful statistics such as word count, character count, sentence count and paragraph count. It is useful when a document, article, assignment or other piece of writing needs to stay within a particular length.

The EasyTools365 Word Counter provides these statistics directly in your browser as you type or paste text. The tool also provides an estimated reading time based on the number of words entered.

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the input area.
  2. Review the word, character, sentence and paragraph statistics shown above the text box.
  3. Check the estimated reading time when you need to understand the approximate length of the text.
  4. Edit your text and the counters will update automatically.
  5. Use Copy Text to copy the current text or Clear to start with an empty input.

When Is a Word Counter Useful?

Word counting can be useful for many types of writing. Students may need to meet assignment limits, while writers and bloggers may want to check the length of articles before publishing them.

Content creators, marketers and editors can also use word and character counts when preparing descriptions, posts, scripts and other material with specific length requirements.

  • School and university assignments
  • Blog posts and articles
  • Product descriptions
  • Reports and business documents
  • Social media content
  • Drafts and other written material

Tips for Checking Your Text

  • Check the word count after finishing a draft so you can see whether it meets the required length.
  • Use the character count when working with platforms that impose character limits.
  • Review sentences and paragraphs to make sure the structure of your text matches your intended format.
  • Treat the displayed reading time as an estimate rather than an exact measurement because reading speed varies between people.
