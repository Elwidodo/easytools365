What Is a YouTube Hashtag Generator?

A YouTube hashtag generator helps creators brainstorm hashtag ideas related to their video topics. Relevant hashtags can provide additional context about content and help organize videos around particular subjects.

EasyTools365 creates suggestions from your topic, selected niche and video type so you can quickly review and choose relevant terms.

How to Generate YouTube Hashtags

  1. Enter your video topic or keywords.
  2. Select YouTube Video or YouTube Shorts.
  3. Choose your niche.
  4. Select 10, 20 or 30 hashtags.
  5. Click Generate Hashtags.
  6. Click an individual hashtag to copy it or use Copy All.
  7. Review the suggestions and keep only hashtags that accurately match your content.

Tips for Choosing YouTube Hashtags

  • Choose hashtags that genuinely describe your video.
  • Combine broad topic terms with specific niche terms when useful.
  • Avoid unrelated or misleading hashtags.
  • Keep your title and description useful independently of hashtags.
  • Review generated suggestions before publishing.
  • For Shorts, use relevant Shorts-related tags only when they fit the content.
