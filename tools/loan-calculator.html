What Is a Loan Calculator?

A Loan Calculator is an online tool that estimates monthly loan payments from the loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment period. It can help you understand the approximate cost of borrowing before comparing loan options.

EasyTools365 performs the calculation directly in your browser, so you can quickly compare different repayment scenarios.

How to Use the Loan Calculator

  1. Enter the amount you want to borrow.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the loan term and choose years or months.
  4. Select your preferred currency.
  5. Click Calculate Loan.
  6. Review the estimated monthly payment, total interest and total payment.

How Loan Payments Are Calculated

For a standard fixed-rate amortizing loan, the payment depends on the principal, periodic interest rate and number of payments. A longer repayment term can lower the monthly payment, but it may increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

If the interest rate is zero, the calculator divides the principal evenly across the number of payments.

Why Compare Loan Terms?

Comparing repayment periods can help you understand the trade-off between monthly affordability and total borrowing cost. A shorter term may mean larger monthly payments but can reduce the amount of interest paid. A longer term may reduce the monthly payment while increasing the total interest.

Use the calculator with several realistic scenarios before comparing actual lender offers.

Important Things to Check Before Taking a Loan

  • Annual interest rate and whether it is fixed or variable.
  • Origination, administration and other lender fees.
  • Taxes, insurance or other costs that may apply.
  • Early repayment conditions and possible penalties.
  • The total amount you will repay, not only the monthly payment.
  • The actual terms and disclosures provided by the lender.
