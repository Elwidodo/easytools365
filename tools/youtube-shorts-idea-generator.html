What Is a YouTube Shorts Idea Generator?

A YouTube Shorts idea generator helps creators brainstorm short-form video concepts for their channels. It can provide starting points for titles, hooks, video concepts and calls to action based on a chosen topic and content style.

EasyTools365 lets you choose a category, style, language and number of ideas, then customize the suggestions for your audience and channel.

How to Generate YouTube Shorts Ideas

  1. Enter your channel niche, keyword or topic.
  2. Select a category such as AI, Anime, Gaming, Food or Facts.
  3. Choose a content style such as Viral, Curiosity or Educational.
  4. Select English or Indonesian.
  5. Choose 10, 20, 30 or 50 ideas.
  6. Click Generate Shorts Ideas.
  7. Review, customize and develop the strongest concepts into your own videos.

Tips for Better YouTube Shorts Ideas

  • Start with a clear topic that matches your channel.
  • Make the opening hook useful, interesting or curiosity-driven.
  • Keep the concept focused on one main idea.
  • Use visual demonstrations whenever they add value.
  • Customize generated ideas instead of publishing generic concepts unchanged.
  • Test different formats and review which topics your audience responds to.
