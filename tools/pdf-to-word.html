About PDF to Word Converter

This free PDF to Word Converter extracts selectable text from PDF pages and creates a real DOCX Word document. The conversion happens in your browser, so the PDF does not need to be sent to a server.

How to Convert PDF to Word

  1. Select a PDF file or drag it into the upload area.
  2. Review the file name, size and page count.
  3. Click Convert to Word.
  4. Wait while the text is extracted page by page.
  5. Click Download Word Document.

What This Tool Preserves

The converter offers two modes. Preserve PDF Layout renders each PDF page into the Word document to keep the visual appearance, spacing, fonts and graphics as close to the PDF as possible. Editable Text extracts selectable text into editable Word paragraphs.

Scanned PDFs and Complex Layouts

A scanned PDF may contain page images instead of actual text. In that case there is no selectable text for PDF.js to extract. Tables, columns, graphics, forms and complex positioning may also require specialized conversion or OCR to reproduce accurately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the PDF to Word Converter free?

Yes. EasyTools365 PDF to Word Converter is free to use online.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion is performed locally in your browser.

Can it convert scanned PDFs?

This version extracts selectable text. Scanned PDFs that contain only images require OCR and may not produce editable text.

Will the original PDF layout be identical in Word?

No guarantee is made for complex layouts. The tool focuses on editable text extraction rather than pixel-perfect PDF reconstruction.
