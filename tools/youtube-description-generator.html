What Is a YouTube Description Generator?

A YouTube description generator helps creators prepare a useful starting draft for video descriptions. A clear description can explain the topic, provide context, naturally include relevant keywords, and give viewers useful information about the video.

EasyTools365 lets you choose a video type, niche and writing style while optionally adding keywords, hashtags and a channel name.

How to Generate a YouTube Description

  1. Enter your video topic or main idea.
  2. Select YouTube Video or YouTube Shorts.
  3. Choose your niche and description style.
  4. Add relevant keywords and an optional channel name.
  5. Choose whether to include a call to action, keywords and hashtags.
  6. Click Generate Description.
  7. Review, customize and copy the generated description.

Tips for Better YouTube Descriptions

  • Describe what the video actually contains.
  • Use important keywords naturally instead of keyword stuffing.
  • Make the opening lines useful because they quickly communicate the video's subject.
  • Add links, credits or resources when they are genuinely relevant.
  • Customize the generated draft to match your own voice and video.
  • Verify facts and claims before publishing.
  • Use only relevant hashtags.
