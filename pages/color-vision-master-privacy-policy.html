1. Introduction

Welcome to Color Vision Master. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our mobile game.

Color Vision Master is a color observation and visual perception puzzle game. We respect your privacy and are committed to providing a transparent explanation of the technologies used by the application.

By using Color Vision Master, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Stored by the App

Color Vision Master may store certain game progress information locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Best Level
  • Best Score
  • Unlocked game levels
  • Other basic game progress required for the game to function

This information is used to provide the game's progress and scoring features.

Game progress is stored locally on your device using the application's local storage functionality. We do not require you to create an account to play the game.

The application may display different types of advertisements, including:

Important: Advertising services may collect and process certain information from your device in order to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, maintain security, and prevent fraud.

This may include:

  • IP address

  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for advertising and fraud prevention

Google states that data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

For more information about Google's handling of data, please review Google's privacy information and advertising policies.
