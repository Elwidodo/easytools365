Welcome to MindTrap: Brain Teaser Games ("MindTrap", "the App", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

By using MindTrap, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Please read it carefully to understand our privacy practices.

1. Information We Collect

MindTrap does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, or phone number to play the game.

Game progress, scores, experience points (XP), Gold Coins, and certain preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Our third-party advertising provider may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • IP address.
  • Device identifiers and advertising ID.
  • Advertising interactions and impressions.
  • App usage and interaction information.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

2. Advertising Services

MindTrap uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The App may display the following advertising formats:

  • Banner Ads: Advertisements displayed within the application.
  • Rewarded Ads: Optional video advertisements that allow users to earn in-game rewards.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen advertisements displayed at appropriate points during gameplay.

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and advertising interactions to provide advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and improve its services.

Google handles information collected through its advertising services according to its own privacy policies.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK Data Disclosure

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through the App and its advertising services may be used for the following purposes:

  • Maintain game functionality and progress.
  • Display advertisements.
  • Provide rewarded advertising features.
  • Improve application performance and stability.
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity.
  • Measure advertising performance.

4. Data Storage and Security

Game progress and preferences may be stored locally on your Android device.

AGS Creative does not operate a user account system or require users to submit personal information to play MindTrap.

You may manage or reset your advertising ID through your Android device settings, where supported.
