Privacy Policy

Brain Glitch

Developer: AGS Creative

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brain Glitch handles information when you use the Brain Glitch mobile game. Brain Glitch is a reaction and puzzle game and does not require users to create an account.

1. Information We Collect

Brain Glitch itself does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or other information that directly identifies you.

The game stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, such as your best score and best level. This information is used only to provide the game's score and progress features.

Local gameplay information is not uploaded by Brain Glitch to our servers.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

Brain Glitch is a free game supported by advertising. We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner and interstitial advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, app and device information, and advertising interaction information for purposes such as delivering, measuring, and improving advertisements, subject to applicable Google policies and user privacy settings.

Brain Glitch does not sell users' personal information. Advertising data handled by Google is subject to Google's own policies.

For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

3. How Information Is Used

Information handled by Brain Glitch or its advertising provider may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the game.
  • Save gameplay progress locally on your device.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical or security issues.
  • Improve advertising and app performance where permitted by applicable settings and laws.

4. Data Sharing

Brain Glitch does not share the locally stored gameplay information with us because it remains on your device.

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Such processing is governed by Google's policies and the settings available to users.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information such as best score and best level is stored locally on your device. You can remove this locally stored information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling Brain Glitch, subject to the behavior of your Android device.

Brain Glitch does not maintain a user account or a server-side user profile that requires an account deletion request.

Information processed by Google for advertising is retained and managed by Google according to Google's applicable policies and retention practices.

6. Children's Privacy

Brain Glitch is not specifically designed or marketed as a child-directed application. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact the developer so that the issue can be reviewed.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. Gameplay records stored by Brain Glitch remain on the user's device. Third-party advertising services use their own security and privacy measures.

8. Third-Party Services

Brain Glitch uses third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), to provide advertising functionality. Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Google privacy information is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy .

9. Your Choices

Depending on your device, region, and Google account settings, you may have controls over advertising personalization and your Android advertising identifier. You can also manage app permissions and app data through Android settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or privacy-related requests regarding Brain Glitch, please use the developer contact information provided on the Brain Glitch Google Play listing.
