Privacy Policy

XOXO – Tic Tac Toe

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

AGS Creative ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and stored when you use the XOXO – Tic Tac Toe mobile application.

Important: XOXO uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and Firebase services to support certain online features.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the App and its service providers may process the following information:

  • Device model and operating system information.
  • App version and technical diagnostic information.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Advertising identifiers and advertising interaction information.
  • Anonymous Firebase authentication identifiers.
  • Online room codes and player connection information.
  • Game statistics, nickname, XP, coins, and preferences.

Game settings and progress may be stored locally on your device. Please do not enter sensitive personal information in your nickname.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the game.
  • Support online multiplayer features.
  • Save game preferences and progress.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Detect fraud and invalid advertising activity.
  • Improve app performance and security.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Advertising and Consent

Advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your location, applicable laws, consent choices, and device settings.

Where required, an appropriate consent mechanism will be provided before processing information for advertising purposes that require consent.

You should never click your own advertisements or encourage others to click advertisements.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Game settings and progress stored locally remain on your device until you clear application data or uninstall the application, subject to device backup settings.

Information processed by Firebase and advertising providers is retained according to their respective privacy policies and retention practices.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers to operate the application, display advertisements, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Information needed for an online room may be visible to other participants in that room.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information processed through the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Data Requests

  • You can stop using online room features at any time.
  • You can clear local application data through device settings.
  • You can uninstall the application to remove locally stored data.
  • You can manage available advertising settings through your device.
  • You can contact us regarding privacy-related requests.

9. Children's Privacy

XOXO is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. The application does not require users to provide their real name, email address, phone number, or home address to play.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

10. International Users

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, subject to applicable data protection laws and safeguards.
