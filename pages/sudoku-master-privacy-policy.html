Sudoku Master – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Master handles information when you use the app.

1. Information stored by Sudoku Master

Sudoku Master stores game progress and settings locally on your Android device. This may include completed games, scores, best times, challenge progress, sound/vibration settings, and dark-mode settings.

This information is stored locally using Android app storage and is not intentionally uploaded to the developer's own server.

2. Advertising

Sudoku Master uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and ad interaction information in accordance with their own policies and applicable consent choices.

3. Third-party services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK provided by Google. For information about Google's privacy practices, please review Google's privacy documentation and advertising settings.

4. Children's privacy

Sudoku Master is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service used by the app, please contact the developer.

5. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may remove locally stored app data by using Android's app settings or by uninstalling Sudoku Master. Advertising and consent choices may also be available through Google's applicable controls.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or third-party services change. The latest version will be published at the same URL used for the app's privacy policy.
