EasyKas Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Application: EasyKas - Simple Cash Management

Developer: EasyTools365

EasyTools365 ("we", "us", or "our") develops and provides the EasyKas - Simple Cash Management application ("EasyKas" or the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and use EasyKas.

We aim to handle user information transparently and responsibly. This policy describes the types of information that may be accessed, stored, processed, or shared through EasyKas and its integrated third-party services.

1. Information Handled by EasyKas

EasyKas is a financial management application that allows users to record and manage income, expenses, balances, transactions, and related financial information.

Information entered by the user may include:

  • Transaction descriptions.
  • Income and expense amounts.
  • Transaction dates.
  • Financial categories.
  • Notes entered by the user.
  • Application settings and preferences.

This information is used to provide the financial management, transaction history, balance, and reporting features of EasyKas.

2. Local Storage

EasyKas stores its core financial records and application information locally on the user's device as part of its normal financial management functionality.

The locally stored information may include transaction records, financial amounts, categories, dates, notes, and application preferences.

EasyTools365 does not provide a user account system or a cloud database for the basic local transaction records described above.

3. Files, Export, and Sharing

EasyKas may provide features for selecting, creating, exporting, or sharing files such as financial reports or transaction data.

When the user intentionally selects a file, exports information, or uses a sharing feature, the selected information may be transferred to the application or service chosen by the user.

EasyTools365 does not control how a third-party application handles information after the user chooses to share it with that application.

4. Notifications

EasyKas may use local notifications to provide reminders and other notification functionality requested or configured by the user.

Notification permissions and settings can be controlled by the user through Android device settings.

5. Advertising

EasyKas may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads, including Google AdMob.

Advertising services may process certain device, advertising, usage, and diagnostic information in order to provide, measure, maintain, and improve advertising services and help prevent fraud.

Depending on the services used and applicable settings, information processed by advertising services may include advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, application interaction information, and diagnostic information.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

More information about Google's privacy practices is available here:

Google Privacy Policy

6. Premium Features and In-App Purchases

EasyKas may provide optional premium features through Google Play in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. EasyTools365 does not intentionally collect or store the user's complete payment card number, bank account number, or other complete payment credentials through EasyKas.

Google Play may process payment and transaction information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

More information about Google's privacy practices is available here:

Google Privacy Policy

7. Third-Party Services

EasyKas may use third-party libraries and services to provide application functionality.

Depending on the features and services used, these may include:

  • Google Mobile Ads / Google AdMob.
  • Google Play Services.
  • Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases.
  • Android notification services.
  • Android file selection and sharing services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. How Information Is Used

Information handled by EasyKas may be used to:

  • Provide financial record management functionality.
  • Calculate balances and financial summaries.
  • Display transaction history.
  • Generate reports.
  • Provide reminders and notifications.
  • Provide optional premium features.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain application functionality.
  • Diagnose and resolve technical problems.
  • Improve application stability and performance.
  • Protect the application against abuse and security threats.

9. Information Sharing

EasyTools365 does not sell users' personal information.

Core financial records stored locally by EasyKas are not intentionally uploaded to an EasyTools365 cloud database as part of the basic local transaction management functionality.

Information may be processed by third-party services when their functionality is used, including advertising services, Google Play purchase services, and Android system services.

When the user intentionally exports or shares information, the selected information may be transferred to the destination application or service selected by the user.

10. Data Security

EasyTools365 takes reasonable measures to maintain the security and reliability of EasyKas.

Core financial records are stored locally on the user's Android device and are subject to the security mechanisms provided by the Android operating system and the user's device.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and device access controls.

11. Data Retention

Financial records and application information stored locally by EasyKas remain on the user's device until the user deletes them, clears the application's data, uninstalls EasyKas, or otherwise removes the information using available application or Android system functionality.

Information processed by third-party services is retained according to the respective third party's privacy policies and terms.

12. Data Deletion

Users can delete financial records using the deletion functionality provided by EasyKas.

Users may also clear EasyKas application data through Android device settings.

Uninstalling EasyKas removes the application's locally stored data from the device, subject to Android operating system behavior and any device backup mechanisms.

If you have questions about information associated with a support request or another communication with EasyTools365, please contact us using the contact information below.

13. Children's Privacy

EasyKas is a general-purpose financial management application and is not specifically directed toward children.

EasyTools365 does not knowingly request personal information from children through EasyKas.

14. External Services and Links

EasyKas and this Privacy Policy may contain links to external websites or services.

EasyTools365 is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or availability of third-party websites or services.

Users should review the privacy policies of third-party services separately.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when EasyKas, its features, third-party services, or our privacy practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or EasyKas, please contact EasyTools365.
