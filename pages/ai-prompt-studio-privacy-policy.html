1. Introduction

AGS Creative ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the AI Prompt Studio mobile application ("the App").

By using AI Prompt Studio, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

AI Prompt Studio does not require you to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, home address, or telephone number to use the main features of the App.

Favorites are stored locally on your device.
The App uses local storage to remember prompts that you save as favorites.

We do not operate a server that stores your favorite prompts as part of the App's normal functionality.

3. Local Storage

AI Prompt Studio uses local device storage to provide features such as saving your favorite prompts.

This information remains on your device unless you remove it by removing the favorite prompt, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App.

We do not use your saved favorites to create a personal account or profile.

Advertising providers may automatically collect certain information about your device and interaction with advertisements in accordance with their own privacy policies.

This information may include device-related information, approximate location information, advertising identifiers, and information about ad interactions.

We do not control the information collected directly by third-party advertising providers.

The App may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, including advertising and application distribution.

6. Prompt Content

AI Prompt Studio provides a library of ready-to-use AI prompts.

The prompts included in the App are stored within the application and are available for you to copy and share.

The App does not require you to submit prompts to our servers in order to use the prompt library.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Favorite prompts stored locally can be removed directly within the App.

You can also remove locally stored App data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App from your device.

Because AI Prompt Studio does not require an online account, there is no user account maintained by us that you need to request deletion of.
