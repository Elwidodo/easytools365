Privacy Policy

MOSQUITO REPELLENT

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MOSQUITO REPELLENT mobile application ("the App"), developed by the app developer ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the App.

1. Information Handled by the App

The App provides audio-frequency generation controls and related timer features.

The App does not require users to create an account, provide their name, or submit contact details to use its main features.

Audio tones are generated directly on your device. The App does not need access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise GPS location for its core functionality.

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements, including banner and interstitial ads.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive certain information from your device to provide, measure, personalize where permitted, and protect advertising services.

Information handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may include:

  • IP address;
  • Device and advertising identifiers;
  • App interactions, including taps and ad views;
  • Advertising activity and performance information;
  • Diagnostic information related to app and SDK performance.

This information may be used for advertising delivery, ad measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

For more information, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK Data Disclosure

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by the App and its advertising services may be used to:

  • Operate the App's features;
  • Maintain app functionality;
  • Display and measure advertisements;
  • Improve advertising performance and security;
  • Prevent fraudulent advertising activity.

4. Data Sharing

We do not operate a service that shares user-submitted personal information with other companies.

However, information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be transmitted to Google and its relevant service providers or advertising partners for the purposes described in Google's policies.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

The App's core audio feature does not send audio generated on your device to us.

We do not maintain user accounts or a developer-operated database of personal information for this App.

Advertising-related information is handled and retained by Google under Google's applicable policies and settings.

You can manage available advertising and privacy controls through your Android device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Advertising services may apply their own age-related and consent settings where required.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the App and our handling of information secure.

Information transmitted by Google Mobile Ads SDK is subject to Google's security practices, including encryption in transit as described in Google's documentation.

You can stop using the App at any time by uninstalling it. You can also review the privacy, advertising, and consent controls available on your Android device.
