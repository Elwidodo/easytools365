ZODIAKMU Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AGS Creative handles information in connection with the ZODIAKMU Android application (package name: com.agscreative.zodiakmu).

1. Information We Collect

ZODIAKMU does not require users to create an account and does not directly ask users to provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, or other personal information.

ZODIAKMU displays daily zodiac and horoscope content. The horoscope content is generated and displayed within the app and does not require users to submit personal information.

2. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

ZODIAKMU uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner and interstitial advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. Depending on the SDK configuration and applicable circumstances, this can include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • user interactions with the app, such as app launches, taps, and ad/video interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • device and account identifiers, including advertising identifiers and app set identifiers where applicable.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Android users can manage or reset their advertising identifier through Android device settings. Some advertising and consent controls may also apply depending on the user's location and applicable regulations.

For more information about Google's handling of data in the Mobile Ads SDK, please review Google's official documentation and privacy information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be used for:

  • delivering and displaying advertisements;
  • measuring advertising performance;
  • analytics and diagnostics;
  • fraud prevention and security.

AGS Creative does not use ZODIAKMU to directly collect personal profile information from users.

4. Information Sharing

ZODIAKMU does not sell users' personal information.

Information processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, measurement, and fraud-prevention services, subject to Google's applicable policies and the user's privacy choices.

5. Data Security

AGS Creative takes reasonable measures to protect information under its control. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google's SDK documentation.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

ZODIAKMU does not maintain user accounts or a user profile database. Therefore, there is no ZODIAKMU account to delete.

Data processed by third-party advertising services is handled according to those providers' policies and retention practices. Users can manage applicable advertising identifiers and privacy choices through their device and Google account settings where available.

7. Children's Privacy

ZODIAKMU is a general-audience horoscope application and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

ZODIAKMU uses the following third-party service:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) — advertising, advertising measurement, analytics, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, data practices, advertising configuration, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this location with a revised effective date.

10. Privacy Contact

For privacy questions or concerns regarding ZODIAKMU, please contact the developer through the contact email provided on the ZODIAKMU Google Play Store listing.

11. More Information

Google's privacy and advertising practices may change over time. Users can review Google's current privacy information and advertising controls through Google's official services.
