This Privacy Policy explains how Easy Video Cutter collects, uses and protects user information.

Information We Access

Easy Video Cutter only accesses video files selected by the user in order to trim or compress videos.

Permissions Used

  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO – Used only to allow users to select video files stored on their device.

How We Use Your Data

  • Trim video files.
  • Compress video files.
  • Save edited videos back to the user's device.

All processing is performed locally on the user's device. No video files are uploaded to our servers.

Data Collection

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not upload user videos.
  • We do not sell user data.
  • We do not share media files with third parties.
